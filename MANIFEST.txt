WHAT CHANGED

  src/data_sets/dsets.py
    ScanSpec.split_frames; CandidateInfoTuple.frame_index (and the hash that
    follows from it); count_frames() and expand_frames(); load_scan_array()
    takes a frame_index; to_model_tensor() keys off array rank; NORMALIZE
    gains a 'shared' mode that keeps whole-acquisition statistics.

  src/training/training.py
    --split-frames flag; build_scan_spec routes split runs to the plain 3D
    network (frame_mode=None, in_channels=1) and warns that --frame-mode is
    then ignored; NORMALIZE choices gain 'shared'; a log line on startup.

  src/inference/make_predictions.py
    get_frame_batch() stacks a scan's frames into one mini-batch; per-frame
    predictions are averaged to one score per scan, so metrics stay
    comparable with pooled runs; NORMALIZE choices gain 'shared'.

  config/arguments.example.txt, README.md
    Document SPLIT_FRAMES and NORMALIZE=shared.

AFTER UPDATING

  Existing behaviour is unchanged with SPLIT_FRAMES unset or false -- verified
  that frame_mode/in_channels routing is identical in that case.

  Split and pooled runs cache under different keys, so no stale tensor can be
  served, but they do not share cache either: a split run re-preprocesses.

  Fold assignments do NOT need rebuilding. Splits are on subject_id, so both
  frames of a scan stay on the same side of the train/validation boundary.
